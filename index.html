What is Niwa Day?

Niwa Day is a desktop app for Windows that gently shows today's schedule as a vertical timeline, floating softly on your desktop. It reads events from your Google Calendar and arranges them from 00:00 to 23:59, with a quiet indicator marking the current time.

No tabs to switch. No window to open. Just look up, and your day is there — ready when you need it, out of the way when you don't.

🪟 Windows 🍎 macOS (planned)

Why only today?

A whole week's worth of events can feel like noise. Niwa Day shows only the day you're actually in — so there's less to scan, less to carry, and more room for what's right in front of you.

One day at a time. That's enough.

Google Calendar Access

Niwa Day connects to Google Calendar as its data source. The connection uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 flow, and we ask only for the minimum permission needed to show your events.

Requested scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly

This scope lets Niwa Day read your calendar events — and nothing more. Niwa Day cannot create, change, or remove any events in your Google Calendar.

How we handle your data

  • Calendar events are fetched in real time and shown only on your device.
  • No calendar data is stored on our servers, cached persistently, or shared with any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are kept safely in the Windows Credential Manager (or macOS Keychain), and are never sent anywhere except to Google's own API.
  • You can revoke Niwa Day's access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

Niwa Day's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. For full details, please see our Privacy Policy.
